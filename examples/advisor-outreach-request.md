# Example: Advisor Outreach Request

```text
Use $phd-advisor-outreach to research this advisor and draft a targeted RA email.

Inputs:
- Candidate CV: /path/to/candidate_cv.pdf
- Advisor: Professor Y
- Advisor homepage: https://example.edu/professor-y
- Target role: long-term RA internship before PhD application
- Candidate direction: multimodal learning, reliable evaluation, and clinical AI
- Tone: concise but strong

Return:
1. Advisor Brief with source links
2. Fit Matrix
3. Email Subject options
4. Email Draft
5. Why This Works
6. Open Risks
```
