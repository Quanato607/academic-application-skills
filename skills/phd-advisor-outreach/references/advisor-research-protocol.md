# Advisor Research Protocol

Use this protocol before writing a targeted PhD, RA, MSc, internship, or visiting-student email.

## Source Order

Use current sources in this order:

1. Official faculty homepage or university profile.
2. Official lab page.
3. Advisor-maintained Google Scholar, DBLP, Semantic Scholar, ORCID, or publication list.
4. Recent paper pages, arXiv, conference proceedings, or lab news.
5. Department admissions pages and lab recruitment notes.
6. News, talks, grants, and social posts only as secondary evidence.

## What To Extract

For each advisor:

- Name, title, institution, department.
- Official email and homepage source.
- Lab name and lab URL if available.
- 3-5 current research topics.
- 2-4 representative recent papers with dates.
- Methods and domains.
- Student output and collaboration network if visible.
- Recruitment signals: explicitly recruiting, application route, no info, not recruiting.
- Uncertainties and stale facts.

## Research Axis Summary

Write the advisor axis as:

> Advisor works on [domain] through [methods], with recent emphasis on [specific thread]. This connects to the candidate through [direct/adjacent bridge].

Do not write:

> Advisor is famous and matches the candidate's interests.

## Current-Fact Rule

Always browse for:

- Email address.
- Recruiting status.
- Latest or recent papers.
- Current title or institution.
- Lab output in the last 1-3 years.

If browsing is unavailable, state that the result is unverified and should not be used as final contact information.
