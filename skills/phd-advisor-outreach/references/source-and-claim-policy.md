# Source And Claim Policy

Use this policy to prevent stale or unsupported outreach claims.

## Source-Backed Claims

Every advisor-specific claim should be backed by at least one source:

- Email and title: official faculty or university page.
- Recruiting status: official lab, department, or advisor statement.
- Recent papers: lab page, Google Scholar, DBLP, arXiv, conference page, or publisher page.
- Lab output: lab publication page, student pages, or proceedings.

## Uncertainty Language

Use clear uncertainty when facts are not confirmed:

- "I did not find a current recruitment statement."
- "The email below is from the official university profile."
- "Based on recent publications, the lab appears to be active in..."
- "This is an adjacent fit rather than a direct fit."

## Claims To Avoid

- "Your lab is the perfect fit" without direct topic match.
- "You are recruiting" without a source.
- "Your latest paper" unless verified by date.
- "I have read all your work" unless true.
- "I can contribute immediately" without concrete evidence.

## Citation In Final Answer

When browsing is used, provide source links in the final response. For email drafts, keep citations outside the email body unless the user asks for a sourced memo.
