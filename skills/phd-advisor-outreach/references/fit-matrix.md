# Fit Matrix

Use this rubric to compare labs and decide what email angle to use.

## Fit Labels

- Direct fit: same research problem, method, dataset, domain, or application setting.
- Strong adjacent fit: different domain but same method or problem structure.
- Adjacent fit: credible transfer but requires careful framing.
- Weak fit: broad-field overlap only.
- No fit: no concrete bridge after reading current work.

## Difficulty Labels

- Reach: elite lab, limited openings, high competition, or advisor not clearly recruiting.
- Target: strong lab and plausible match with a concrete bridge.
- Safer: less saturated target or clear RA/intern opening.
- Unknown: insufficient recruitment or output information.

## Matrix Fields

For every advisor, fill:

- Fit level.
- Difficulty.
- Entry path: PhD, RA, MSc pre-entry intern, visiting, summer intern.
- Advisor hook: one paper, project, or lab thread.
- Candidate angle: one evidence-backed profile point.
- Proposed bridge: one possible collaboration direction.
- Risk: missing facts, weak overlap, no recruitment signal, overcompetitive lab.
- Priority: high, medium, low.

## Scoring

Use 1-5 scores.

- Topic match.
- Method match.
- Domain match.
- Candidate evidence strength.
- Lab output.
- Application feasibility.

Overall:

- 4.3-5.0: top priority.
- 3.6-4.2: good target.
- 3.0-3.5: possible but needs careful framing.
- Below 3.0: low priority unless user has a special reason.
